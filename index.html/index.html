    Frequently asked questions

    How is the price decided?

    After an inspection we prepare a written quotation based on size, condition and the services you pick. The estimate on this page is indicative only.

    Do I pay in advance?

    You pay 50% when you approve the quotation and 50% on delivery.

    Which areas do you serve?

    We start in selected districts of Cairo and Giza: New Cairo, Sheikh Zayed / 6th of October, Maadi, Nasr City / Heliopolis and Mohandessin / Dokki.

    Does the scanner replace an inspection?

    No. The scanner gives an initial view from photos only; the on-site inspection confirms scope and safety.

    What if the apartment needs major repairs?

    Major repairs and renovation are quoted separately and need your approval before any work starts.
